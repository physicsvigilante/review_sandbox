## Findings

### F-01 — MINOR (non-blocking): boundary-power sign deserves an explicit teacher-facing explanation

- **Category / source:** Educational interpretation; [viewer/index.html lines 63–66](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/sim_heat/blob/e8449989ba55a972976ebba7e9708bc4e2351a18/viewer/index.html#L63-L66), [trace.mjs lines 35–39](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/sim_heat/blob/e8449989ba55a972976ebba7e9708bc4e2351a18/viewer/trace.mjs#L35-L39), historical `case_01/SimHeat.mo` in artifact 14155084346 (the fixed-temperature source defines `Q_to_network = -source.port.Q_flow`).
- **Reproduction / observed:** At saved `t=1000 s`, the original CSV gives `cooled.Q_to_network = -11.050441583485814 W`. The screenshot correctly shows **Cold source · W: −11.05 W**. The UI explains that the value is instantaneous W, but does not explicitly explain the **boundary power sign** (positive = boundary supplies heat to network; negative = network supplies heat to boundary). A teacher could interpret “cold source” as only a supplier.
- **Expected / correction:** The value, sign and unit are correct; no physics fix. Optionally add a small note to the energy card, such as “Signed boundary power: + to network; − absorbed from network,” or label the cold boundary as a sink/maintained reservoir.
- **Evidence / confidence / regression:** Source inspection, native CSV and read-only author screenshot; **high confidence** in labeling ambiguity, **no numerical defect**. Suggested regression: verify the sign explanation and negative cold boundary value together at `t=1000 s`. Not a merge blocker.
