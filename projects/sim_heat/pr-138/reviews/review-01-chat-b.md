## Temperature and energy

Positive rod conductivity makes the transformed temperature increase. Each passive coupling and enclosure radiation therefore gives a decreasing net heat input as body temperature increases. Once the motion has a definite direction, the entry upper speed gives a lower panel residence time, and a positive exit lower speed gives an upper residence time. The implementation rounds those times outward. It also bounds partial-panel displacement using an upper bound on remaining time and entry speed. A panel with nonpositive upper exit speed can contain an asymptote. With uncertain initial sign the two-sided initial-speed enclosure and the extreme bath temperatures suffice. Multiplying displacement by the positive capacity preserves the energy-bound ordering.
