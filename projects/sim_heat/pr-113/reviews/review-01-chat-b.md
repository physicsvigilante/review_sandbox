## Uncertain exhaustion time

I changed the revised bridge feed conductivity from 40 to 30 W/(m K) in temporary model and facts files and rebound their hashes. The first temporary descriptor accidentally referenced itself as its parent and the loader refused it. I then used a standalone descriptor for this independent example. For unit drive the junction equations are 6u − v = 3 and −u + 3v = 1. Their solution gives G = 29/17 W/K. A 17 K difference transfers exactly 29 W, so equal budgets of 290 J yield a 10 s event. With 280 K and 297 K baths in both orders, the real solver's outward rate bounds strictly enclosed the signed analytical value and the event interval straddled 10 s. At 9, 10 and 11 s the tool returned coexisting, exhaustion_time_uncertain and exhausted respectively. The reported energies enclosed ±261 J at 9 s and ±290 J at both later horizons. This exercised the public calculation with a real network and no patched interval. The committed coupled-bath tests cover exact rates. Preserving a nonzero-width interval example as a future regression would extend coverage.
