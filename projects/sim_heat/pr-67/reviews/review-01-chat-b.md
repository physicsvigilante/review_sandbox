## R2 conclusion and physical/source argument

**No blocking defect identified within the advertised bounded algebraic scope.** N6 builds the unknown-junction block of the weighted graph Laplacian from source-exact positive `G=kA/L` for `RodArea`. Each oriented physical rod contributes `q_ab=G(T_a-T_b)`, `+q_ab` outward at `a`, and `-q_ab` at `b`. The junction equation is `sum(q_out)=0`; the diagonal is the sum of incident `G`, off-diagonal entries are `-G`, and known body/boundary neighbors contribute `G*T_known` to the right-hand side. Parallel physical rods correctly contribute independently; reversed orientations do not change the Laplacian. [N6 assembly, pinned lines 119–159](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/sim_heat/blob/4517bf8e14f659e79255bc1d20f7fcb7b72ce695/qualification/h1_n6/exact_junctions.py#L119-L159).

For any vector on unknown nodes, `xᵀAx = sum_unknown-unknown G(x_i-x_j)^2 + sum_unknown-known G*x_i^2` (each physical edge counted once). N4 refuses an unanchored connected component and open ownerless ports; bodies and fixed boundaries are known nodes in N6. Thus every unknown connected component has a positive-conductance path to a known temperature, so `A` is symmetric positive definite and the Dirichlet solution is **unique** without an invented reference temperature. N6 checks every exact positive elimination pivot, the reduced identity matrix, and the **original** equations with `Fraction`, refusing on failure. [N4 topology](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/sim_heat/blob/4517bf8e14f659e79255bc1d20f7fcb7b72ce695/qualification/h1_n4/static_physical.py#L145-L201); [N6 elimination](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/sim_heat/blob/4517bf8e14f659e79255bc1d20f7fcb7b72ce695/qualification/h1_n6/exact_junctions.py#L61-L98); [residual check](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/sim_heat/blob/4517bf8e14f659e79255bc1d20f7fcb7b72ce695/qualification/h1_n6/exact_junctions.py#L156-L183).

The maximum principle follows component by component from positive conductances and zero unknown-node outgoing flux; an interior strict maximum/minimum beyond that component's anchors would contradict the nodal average equation. The implementation directly checks the **weaker global known-temperature envelope**, while exact residual checks plus positive `G` establish the stronger component-wise mathematical result. This is correct; a per-component executable assertion could be additional defense in depth, not a prerequisite for this scoped pass.

Energy/sign check: the code adds each physical `q` once at its from-node and `-q` at its to-node; junction residuals are verified **individually** zero. It defines `C*dT/dt = -Q_out(body)` and outward-positive `Q_supply(boundary)=Q_out(boundary)`. Accordingly `sum_bodies C*dT/dt = sum_boundaries Q_supply` at the assumed instant. The returned global `sum_node Q_out=0` is a **structural instantaneous antisymmetry identity**, **not** proof of time-integrated energy conservation, native fidelity or an IVP trajectory.
