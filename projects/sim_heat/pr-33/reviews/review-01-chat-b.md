### F-03 — MINOR / HARDENING: decompressed output bound is not a decoder-memory bound

**Source:** [`inspect_pr31_fixture.py` lines 77–90](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/sim_heat/blob/b8c5152aac86fc1be32f7b98e032f108d8345c66/qualification/h1_c1/immutable_fixture/inspect_pr31_fixture.py#L77-L90).
