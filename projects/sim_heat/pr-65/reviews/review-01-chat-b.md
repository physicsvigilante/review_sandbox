## Findings requiring changes

### R2-01 — BLOCKER: ownerless degree-one endpoint passes graph admission

[Source lines 125–175](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/sim_heat/blob/a704e100d6760423b08cb07eca131ada8a629db2/qualification/h1_n4/static_physical.py#L125-L175) add rod endpoints to `used` and require the overall connected component to intersect `owned`. Neither condition proves **each rod port participates in an explicit connection**. The [H1 contract, §4](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/sim_heat/blob/a704e100d6760423b08cb07eca131ada8a629db2/docs/H1_IMPLEMENTATION_CONTRACT.md#L112-L124) explicitly refuses unintentionally open rod ends.

**Executed counterexample:** A `steady` source with `nodes=["Hot","Free"]`, one `Thermostat` boundary owning `Hot`, no bodies, and one `RodArea DanglingRod(a="Hot",b="Free",k=12,L=0.5,A=0.001)`. Other mandatory fields: valid `schema="sim_heat.reference/1"`, `id="OpenEndContradictsH1"`, `analysis={}`, nonempty `provenance`, and syntactically valid 64-hex `modelSha256`/`studySha256`. The compact sorted JSON plus trailing newline is SHA-256 **`6e04545c54689ce5994604ea14cf33ec874f80c90f6a92a16225392c95efc3d7`** when the two metadata digests are respectively `"1"*64` and `"2"*64`.

**Actual:** `create_static_candidate` returns `DanglingRod.G = 0x1.89374bc6a7efap-6`, `physical100x="PASS_CANDIDATE_NOT_NATIVE_QUALIFIED"` (conductance 3/125 W/K). **Required:** `PhysicalRefusal` for unattached endpoint `Free`. A second independently executed **transient** case (one body and circular rod terminating at an ownerless degree-one node) also incorrectly succeeds with `Plate.C`, `Finned.A`, `Finned.G`.

**Correction:** Count actual rod-port incidences at every synthetic node. Unowned nodes must have at least two rod endpoints; owner nodes may have one, and a degree-zero *body* remains a valid isolated body. Keep boundary connectivity and per-component anchoring rules. Add negative steady/transient degree-one tests and positive degree-two junction, parallel-rod, loop, and isolated-body regressions. Do not fill an open end with a fictitious insulating boundary or change the physics.
