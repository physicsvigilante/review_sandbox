## Physics and refusal checks

For directed net power toward the target, the transit integral for 1 J/K scales with total capacity. If positive unit passage bounds are [a,b] and observed times are [L,H], the combined capacity bounds in J/K are [L/b,H/a] in the chosen SI units. Subtracting the tare in J/K and dividing by positive mass in kg preserves order and produces J/(kg K). The implementation uses rational arithmetic and requires a reached, positive-duration passage. Refusals cover indistinct and asymptotic targets and invalid timing or capacity inputs.
