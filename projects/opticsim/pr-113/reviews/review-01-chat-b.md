## 3. Independent physical reference — actual finite hits, no prescribed itinerary

The [fixture](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/94ea1a184e025065fe28da49691a3f39dc916296/examples/native-thick-lens-mirror-v16.json) specifies S1=(−200,10) mm pointing +x, L1=(0,0), aperture=100 mm, centre thickness=30 mm, Rfront=Rback=100 mm, inside/outside indices 1.5/1, explicit Fresnel-off; finite M1 centred (130,0), tangent +90°, length 200 mm; and two-sided D1 centred (−100,0), tangent +90°, length 240 mm. Use x-right/y-up coordinates throughout.

**Independent construction:** With half-aperture a=50 and left/right vertex positions L=−15, H=+15 mm, the actual finite spherical faces are portions of circles centred at **(85,0)** and **(−85,0)** with radius 100 mm. The front and back x coordinates for valid |y|≤50 are x_front(y)=85−sqrt(10000−y²) and x_back(y)=−85+sqrt(10000−y²). Their two finite horizontal rim segments end at x=±1.602540378443864, y=±50. At *each* propagated segment I solved for all positive intersections with **both finite circular arcs, both finite rims, the complete mirror segment and the detector**, selected the smallest positive travel parameter, then applied local-normal Snell transmission or the exact specular reflection vector. Recomputed rays were not chosen from an expected face-ID sequence. At an arc with outward unit normal n, the tangential invariant is n_incident × (incoming × n) = n_destination × (outgoing × n), with the appropriate crossing-side sign; for the plane mirror the outgoing direction is incoming−2(incoming·normal)normal. Neither Opticsim nor native PhyDemo code was imported into this mathematical reference.

## 5. Ranked residual findings and reproducible adversarial follow-ups

**R2-V16-01 — P2 numerical evidence gap, nonblocking: a mirror-directed *return* can be aimed at an actual lens rim/arc corner even though the initial source ray passed the v15 near-corner check.**

A reproducible **reviewer-derived, mathematically corner-targeting fixture** is the unmodified baseline v16 physical example with **M1.orientationDeg = 76.18094629718023°** instead of 90°, all other fields unchanged. My finite-line calculation places the mirror collision at **(129.499306498930, −2.035536367814)**, with reflected unit direction **(−0.926270795740, +0.376858611364)**, aimed at the right/top lens corner **(1.602540378444,+50)**, where the back arc meets the top rim. The mirror's entire segment is well outside the lens x-extent; this is admitted by the documented source/mirror/lens geometry restrictions. Its incidence at a sharp corner is *physically ambiguous*, not a certified refractive branch. Nearby angle changes above/below this value produce different hit/miss topology.

**Assessment:** v15's authoring-time tangent/corner guard checks the *initial emitted ray*, not a future reflected segment, as expected for a simulator that refuses to prescribe a path. The unchanged spherical-lens event observer marks a callback within **0.01 mm** of a corner **invalid**; the ordinary invalid-observation error and completion gate then withhold accepted measurements. But if native floating-point geometry *skips* the borderline contact, there is no proof from current source-native tests that it will refrain from qualifying a misleading clean miss. **This configuration was independently derived; I did not execute it with PhyDemo and do not claim a native defect.**

**Suggested bounded test:** in a new native-only regression, replay this exact fixture and, e.g., **76.177° and 76.185°** as adjacent controls, logging raw native hits, completion classification and public results. Assert no invented return or accepted ambiguous face identity; for any actually observed near-corner callback, results must be withheld. Preserve native thresholds and do not introduce an implicit analytical route. This is a recommended stronger numerical envelope, **not** grounds to claim presently incorrect completed output.

**R2-V16-02 — P2 near-coincident availability/documentation gap, nonblocking: ordinary mirror and detector segments have no global 0.01-mm mutual clearance rule.**

Reproducible **admission probe:** add D2 of type ray-detector to the baseline, with **x=130.0000001 mm, y=0, orientationDeg=90°, length=200 mm**, leaving existing M1 at x=130, y=0, orientation=90°, length=200 and D1 unchanged. The two distinct parallel segments are separated by **10⁻⁷ mm**, so they do not intersect; their source supporting lines and lens x-extents are also safe. The inherited [finiteSurfacesConflict](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/94ea1a184e025065fe28da49691a3f39dc916296/src/composition-validation.js#L57-L62) uses ordinary segment intersections, **not** an aperture-style positive-distance band, for this mirror/detector pair. The validator is therefore structurally capable of admitting this separation. Pinned [Simulator.MIN_RAY_SEGMENT_LENGTH](https://github.com/ricktu288/ray-optics/blob/811f508d2b070898c241a568b0c167dcb8645251/src/core/Simulator.js#L49-L53) is **10⁻⁶ mm** in native scene units, larger than this example's gap. Depending on ray order/conditioning, native may report near-simultaneous undefined behavior and then correctly suppress public measurements. **No reviewer-native outcome is asserted here.**

**Suggested correction:** explicitly distinguish *lens–mirror* certified 0.01-mm complete-x separation from *mirror–detector* **intersection-only legacy validation** in the v16 prose. Add this fixture to native/Chrome regression with assertions that any native undefined/partial outcome cannot publish qualified detector/mirror results. Do **not** silently alter the shared v4 detector predicate, since that would broaden a v16 review into changed historical admission semantics. This is a real scope/precision clarification and bounded coverage recommendation; no independently reproduced wrong result has been found.

## 6. Numerical and educational scope boundaries

The pinned native default-color Fresnel cutoff, intrinsic small-segment threshold, processed-ray budget, interaction-depth ceiling and known grazing/critical-angle precision limitations are inherited, not repaired in v16. A **0.01-mm admission clearance is geometric**, not a proven global double-precision ray-intersection error bound. Fresnel-off means explicit transmitted-only approximation (native TIR remains), not complete Fresnel physics; full Fresnel may correctly yield **incomplete/suppressed** results. Stage-1 optional finite-hit diagnostic concerns an isolated v9 polygonal Glass and remains **noncertifying**; its prospective Stage-2 mandatory rules are not adopted by this v16 milestone.

A successful complete native result establishes **sampled native interactions only**. Four lens encounters plus one mirror reflection do not establish image formation, detector-source ancestry for multiple rays, continuous intensity, electromagnetic wave phase, dispersion, time-of-flight, 3D optics or a correct interpretation of an original JEE problem. These limitations are accurately represented in v16 authored capabilities and observable warnings.
