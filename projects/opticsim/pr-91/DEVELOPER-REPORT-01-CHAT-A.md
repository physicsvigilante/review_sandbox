- The genuine OFF/ON distribution under randomized **fixed** fresh-process schedules confirms observable same-input critical native behavior variability even without an opt-in diagnostic: translated OFF includes native `failed` and `completed` executions; rotated OFF includes 4/6 actual Glass callback counts. The present small trial difference **does not prove observer causation or neutrality**. ON/OFF are still different native executions and cannot be compared as the same individual ray object.
