### F3 — Conservative and coherent source, boundary, detector and blocker admission: PASS

[Validator lines 20–73](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/635fc28d48d4ebf5408ce5a45988b7831da6a81b/src/native-curved-entry-observation.js#L20-L73) require: one exact horizontal right-to-left SingleRay; source x in [centre + 1.2R, centre + 4R], |source y − axis| ≤ 0.08R, at least 10 mm from the true incident arc; radius **50–1000 mm**, fully inside the world; explicit indices **1–4**, inside strictly higher than outside; and explicit **partialReflection=false**. This is a clearly declared transmitted-only Fresnel approximation, not a suppression of the native total-internal-reflection solver.

Both passive detectors must be vertical, centred on the material axis and positioned at **0.55–0.9R**. One opaque blocker is vertical at **0.2–0.5R**, behind both detector planes but in front of the genuine flat face. Each segment's finite endpoints must be strictly inside the circular medium with at least **1 mm** glass-boundary clearance, and the axial detector/detector/blocker spacing must exceed **max(1 mm, 0.05R)**. The half-disk is convex, so in-region endpoints on a vertical chord imply the entire segment is in-region. The rule forbids surface coincidences; it does not predict the native encounter order.

The near-axis bound also has a reassuring independent geometric margin: |incident transverse angle| ≤ arcsin(0.08) ≈ 0.0801 rad; since the first passage is from lower to higher n, its transmitted axial deflection cannot exceed that angle. Before the earliest admissible screen plane, |y − axis| is bounded conservatively by approximately **0.08R + 0.8R·tan(arcsin 0.08) < 0.145R**, while the shortest screen or detector half-length is **0.25R**. Thus under ideal Snell propagation the full admissible forward ray crosses the supplied segments without grazing their endpoints. This is a bound for the supported family, not a claim about arbitrary other optical systems or pathological numerical failures. Observed native events, not this bound, remain the reported measurements.

The [public v24 tests](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/635fc28d48d4ebf5408ce5a45988b7831da6a81b/test/jee-curved-entry-v24.test.mjs#L127-L160) reject absent indices/Fresnel declarations, wrong-way or wrong-mode source, inverted/equal index, source starting inside glass, rim excursion, excessive segment length, out-of-glass or rotated detector, detector overlap, missing/misplaced screen, and extra optics. The [11-case public acceptance](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/635fc28d48d4ebf5408ce5a45988b7831da6a81b/scripts/jee-curved-entry-acceptance.mjs#L43-L96) contains **four actual complete native traces** and **seven strict pre-native refusals with zero native calls**. It neither guesses missing apparatus data nor repairs a rejected request.
