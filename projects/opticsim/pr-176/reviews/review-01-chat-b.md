## 3. New v23 finite Beam + true native CircleGlass physics — PASS

**Authored/native correspondence.** [`src/native-circle-beam.js`](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/4ffc44e513d00c4b4ef7fa284d867c0cc0cbd647/src/native-circle-beam.js), [`src/circular-dielectric.js`](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/4ffc44e513d00c4b4ef7fa284d867c0cc0cbd647/src/circular-dielectric.js) and [`src/parallel-beam.js`](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/4ffc44e513d00c4b4ef7fa284d867c0cc0cbd647/src/parallel-beam.js) preserve one finite deterministic `Beam`, a genuine full `CircleGlass` (both curved interfaces), and zero to two passive finite `Detector` objects, **not** an image proxy or invented tracing algorithm. The canonical y-up-to-native y-down transform is explicit. The normalized native index ratio is `insideIndex/outsideIndex`; both absolute indices are still required. `partialReflection=false` explicitly omits Fresnel partial-reflection branches, **not** native TIR.

**Admission envelope.** Exactly one Beam/one circle; full circle extent inside ±10,000 mm; radius at least 20 mm; explicit indices [1,4] in either order; horizontal 0/±180° source; perpendicular width 10–100 mm; whole source segment external and ≥0.01 mm before the near tangent plane and strictly inside 90% radius transverse strip; native deterministic sample count 1–100; ray density 0.048–1 with Beam brightness ≤ density; 0–2 distinct vertical downstream detectors ≥0.01 mm beyond the far tangent plane. Unlike the old v7 single-ray circle, this policy admits no source inside the region, tangential initial beam, extra optics or Fresnel weak branches. Initial **outside-higher-index** samples are refused at or within `1e-5` of `n_outside · |impact|/R >= n_inside`, a necessary first-interface critical-angle check, **not** an invented transmitted ray.

**Independent Snell and detector calculation.** From the archived synthetic forward case (`R=100 mm`, Beam source `x=-160`, width `20`, density `0.2`, `n_out=1`, `n_in=1.5`), native midpoint sampling is four rays at `y=±7.5, ±2.5 mm`. The builder native observation reports 4.301222304670365° and 2.865983982598852°, consistent within rounding. Vector refraction predicts the first interior propagation direction approximately **`(0.9996862743168, -0.0250470146449)`**, matching its native outgoing direction.

Using **independently computed entry, second circle intersection, second Snell refraction and straight detector intersection** at `x=260 mm`, the four predicted detector y-intercepts are **`-5.527296643273`, `-1.834340725095`, `+1.834340725095`, `+5.527296643273 mm`**, all matching the archived genuine-native sample positions.
