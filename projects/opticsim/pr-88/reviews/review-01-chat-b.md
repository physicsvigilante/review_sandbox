## Required design changes / blockers

### B1 — **BLOCKER / high: mandatory policy ahead of a defensible observer-neutrality design**

The [proposal § observer](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/docs/NATIVE-FINITE-FORWARD-QUALIFICATION-PROPOSAL.md#L22-L36) wisely requires one owning callback and original native return/reference identity, but the research [`nativeObservation`](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/evaluation/finite-hit-forward-qualification-v1/shadow.mjs#L58-L87) uses a disposable second wrapper around the existing observer. It does **not** demonstrate that a new mandatory production hook leaves this near-critical native execution behavior-neutral. Existing [`observeInterfaceSet`](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/src/planar-media.js#L53-L104) itself mutates only its meter while delegating one native call, but that alone is not a general neutrality proof.

**Smallest remedy:** revise the normative rollout to **test-only / opt-in diagnostic first**, with no silent upgrade of affected native measurements to “proven correct.” Specify an eventual *single owner* integrating pre-mutation scalar capture; no BigInt calculations in the hot native callback (post-run on bounded snapshots instead); try/finally or equivalent guaranteeing one native delegation despite audit errors; preserve `this`, argument references, original ray mutation and returned/newRays **object identities**. Add same-run assertions for callback ownership, pre/post values and references, callback-to-native-meter counts, and native terminal/branch/truncation evidence. Test normal and far-from-critical control parity; repeatedly alternate order/warm-up for critical cases, **record** rather than demand equal separate-run critical trajectories. If the impact cannot be bounded, keep diagnostic opt-in or explicitly unavailable for the exposed cases; do not make mandatory suppression a default merely on the basis of 11 green workflows. Owner must approve the policy change explicitly after a revised R2 design/evidence check.

### B2 — **BLOCKER / high: accepted forward face crossing does not necessarily validate the reported native incident POINT**

Current research [`faceEvidence`](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/evaluation/finite-hit-forward-qualification-v1/shadow.mjs#L118-L155) (and the proposed rule) grounds an **engine-supplied hit point** to one edge, then proves that the snapshotted ray intersects that edge *somewhere* forward with exact `0≤u≤1` and sufficient step. **It never checks the computed intersection point against the native-reported hit point.** Therefore the statement “all actual native finite hits have a represented-forward finite intersection” can overstate what was checked.

**Independent mathematical counterexample (not a claim that native produces it):** segment `(10,0)→(10,10)`; ray `(0,5)→(1,5)`; callback falsely reports `(10,7)`. Unique face, vertex clearance and native distance all pass. Exact intersection is `(10,5)`, `t=10>0`, `u=0.5`, beyond native cutoff, so the current predicate returns **clean** despite a wrong incident point. Similarly, an exact intersection at a **finite-edge endpoint** (`u=0` or `u=1`) could be accepted if the reported point is away from the vertex; vertex exclusion is currently performed only on reported point.

**Smallest remedy:** (a) compare independently computed exact intersection with actual callback point using an explicitly justified and independently adversarially tested numerical tolerance or uncertainty window; (b) apply vertex/corner ambiguity to the *computed intersection* as well as the reported point; (c) classify tolerance-borderline cases **unknown**, not native-proven-defective. Keep exact rational sign, cutoff and finite `u` tests; do not demand bitwise-identical floating hit coordinates. If incident-coordinate agreement is intentionally excluded, explicitly weaken the qualifier's name/claimed meaning to “a separate forward crossing exists on the selected face,” not validated native-hit geometry.

### B3 — **BLOCKER / high: atomic qualified-publication matrix is not specified end-to-end**

The proposed [publication paragraph](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/docs/NATIVE-FINITE-FORWARD-QUALIFICATION-PROPOSAL.md#L38-L51) names the right surfaces but does not define a testable end-to-end outcome matrix, particularly where **native completed** but geometry is inconsistent or audit unavailable. In unchanged production [`checkCandidate`](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/skills/sim-ro-image-to-scene/scripts/check-scene.mjs#L53-L82), `executionPassed` follows completion, source/detector checks and round trips; [`evaluateDocument`](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/scripts/author-scene.mjs#L30-L56) publishes `summary.observations` whenever native completed; [`evaluateObservables`](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/src/observables.js#L68-L149) trusts that `executionPassed`; [`runProblemEvidence`](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/scripts/run-problem.mjs#L63-L95) saves native evidence and derives coverage/exit code; and [browser stats/detector UI](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/src/app.js#L71-L111) displays raw detector power on native-completed status alone. Those are **existing integration obligations**, not bugs introduced by PR #88 (which has no production code).

**Smallest remedy:** define one revision-bound shared qualification envelope, with **separate immutable native terminal status**, audit status `checked/checked-no-hit/inconsistent/unavailable/not-applicable`, stable machine reason, and **independent measurement qualification**. For inconsistent/unavailable v9 cases, `summary.observations` and every *trusted* detector crossing/power, interface first/angles/count/face event, selected observable, comparison, result coverage and qualified export must be null/suppressed/unavailable **atomically**. Do not rely on native.status or a v9 `executionPassed=true` to imply measurement fitness. `source-emissions`, processed counts and diagnostics may remain as **explicitly raw, unqualified** run evidence, not accepted downstream optics. Preserve the separately valid authored-scene export, truthful native `completed`, historical non-v9 shapes, and independent analytical calculations. Specify the treatment of `native-evidence.json`, CLI process exit/coverage, portable packages and reimports, browser raw stats and the no-request result path. Require **A→B→A** delayed terminal/audit completion tests with revision *sequence*, not matching SHA alone; [`Session`](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/src/session.js#L19-L46) and [`QualifiedUI`](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/src/qualified-ui.js#L151-L175) already supply mechanisms to reuse.

### B4 — **MAJOR / release blocker if kept mandatory: no admissible-domain coverage for 128 hits and Fresnel branches**

The [proposed cap](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/docs/NATIVE-FINITE-FORWARD-QUALIFICATION-PROPOSAL.md#L30-L36) correctly fails closed beyond 128, without halting native propagation or treating a clipped audit as clean. But the research [24-case generator](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/evaluation/finite-hit-forward-qualification-v1/shadow.mjs#L20-L55) uses **exactly one source, zero detectors and `partialReflection:false`**, and final cases observe at most **five** callbacks each. Existing v9 allows **1–4 sources, 0–2 detectors, full Fresnel:true, depth up to 8 and processed limit up to 10,000** ([contract](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/src/contract.js#L140-L144)). There is no evidence that 128 does not make reasonable completed multi-source/Fresnel scenes unqualifiable or block the browser. Nor were the missing-callback/over-cap negative paths executed in this study.

**Smallest remedy:** explicitly label the cap a **policy availability limit**, not physics failure; prove zero silent clean overflow at 128/129 and separate post-cap total count, then run bounded actual-native source/detector/Fresnel matrices including long-lived weak branches and noncompletion. Capture time/memory/browser responsiveness for worst admitted scenes. Consider cheaper exact determinant sign/range comparisons (cross-multiplied dyadic integers, only compute/display rational intersection coordinates when needed), with a bound on arithmetic and payload size. Set the cap from measured legitimate workloads or retain an honest `audit-unavailable` result; do not relax upstream depth/truncation/threshold or silently classify overflow clean.

### B5 — **MAJOR: complete callback and source/observer ownership needs non-circular negative tests**

The proposal requires every actual Glass incident, source start, and exact instance ID. But counting the audit hook and existing [interface meter](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/src/planar-media.js#L60-L104) inside the same wrapper is not independent proof against a bypassed or replaced native method. A zero callback tally can be truly no-hit **or** failed installation. One 4-vertex scene does not imply all multiple-source/detector interleavings were seen.

**Smallest remedy:** certify installed callback identity and single delegation before emission and at terminal, record bounded source start/count provenance per authored ID, detect wrapper changes and malformed/missed hooks, and supply controlled negative tests (missing/duplicate ownership, unexpected native instance, bypassed hook, zero-hit with no installed hook, bad ray/incident, invalid arithmetic, 128/129). Zero-hit must be `checked-no-hit` *only when* installed/started and terminal/counter agreements are proven. Do not introduce per-source path attribution or a persistent ray graph.

## Design judgments, scope limits, and nonblockers

- **Sound core principle:** Exact rational arithmetic on the *actual pre-mutation binary64 ray* and exact native/ authored finite face, with **strict positive step, greater-than pinned squared cutoff, and finite `u∈[0,1]`**, is a useful **necessary representation-level** condition. For `t=0`, the current exact helper's field `forward` uses `t≥0`, but `beyondCutoff` correctly rejects the zero step. Rename/explicitly test this if promoting it; it is not demonstrated false acceptance in current shadow code ([exact helper](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/28c4aa94bbbf6b5a63a8bef433c80186e08a3d35/scripts/exact-line-reference.mjs#L32-L44)).
- **Conservative distinction:** nonforward, subcutoff, outside finite segment or nonintersecting represented lines can justify *withholding this run's trusted measurements*; they do **not** prove the intended real-world critical branch impossible or mandate a PhyDemo patch. Near vertices, degenerate exact lines, callback ambiguity and inability to observe are **unknown/unavailable**, not accused optical defects. A genuine forward very-short step **above** cutoff, near-parallel forward intersection, exactly finite endpoint and near-vertex cases need independent expected classification to avoid false refusal; an endpoint may reasonably become `unknown` under existing 0.01 mm policy, not blanket `inconsistent`.
- **Clean is narrow:** absence of a detected finite-forward contradiction is not proof of Snell validity, correct native Fresnel branching, numerical convergence, source interpretation, all unrecorded ray history or real-world optical result. No native-completed status should be silently rewritten as failed. Genuine trapped circle TIR/truncation is separate and unchanged.
- **Nonblocker / desirable:** keep anonymous bounded event-reason counts for auditability (without publishing an unqualified path), provenance SHA and original first-negative artifacts, explicit sampled power units, source-independent attribution, exact authored/native face coordinate binding and normalization/units tests across scales/rotations. Preserve v5/v7/v8 and other schemas unchanged; do not expand this guard to every optics family.
