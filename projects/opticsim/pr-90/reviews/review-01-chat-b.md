## 4. Findings, evidence limits and decision

**F-01 — PASS: narrow descriptive repeatability is genuinely supported.** Scene-hash-preserved OFF runs already vary: translated runs have both `completed` and `failed`; rotated runs have 4, 5 and 6 native Glass callbacks while all report completed. The experiment therefore shows **between-execution native numerical behavior sensitivity at these particular synthetic computed-critical representations**. This does not identify an upstream root cause or show that all near-critical configurations are unstable.

**F-02 — No causal inference or neutrality conclusion (limitation; blocker to any proposed policy/physics inference).** ON and OFF are **different native executions**, not an identical-ray counterfactual; the 7/10 vs 5/10 translated success counts are small and serially/within-process dependent. ABBA is deterministic counterbalancing, **not random assignment or control of JIT, process history, native global state, memory layout or ray scheduling**. Similar rotated distributions do not prove observer neutrality, and their differences do not prove observer interference. No significance or power estimate treating these 20 as independent random samples would resolve the confounding.

**F-03 — Represented-line diagnostic output is not independent optical certification (important evidence boundary).** `inconsistent / EXACT_INTERSECTION_NOT_FORWARD` is an existing Stage-1 claim of a necessary **represented finite-forward callback geometry** violation on a completed observed trace, not a verdict on all rays or on the mathematical critical branch. Conversely `checked` for the two normal controls is only a necessary consistency check, not full physical validation; `unknown` is neither proof of correctness nor proof of a bad oblique ray. **The retained artifact contains per-run verdicts, counters and first native angles, but not the original per-incident binary64 ray endpoints and native incident-point coordinates.** Therefore I could verify status accounting and the documented logic, **but could not independently rerun exact `BigInt` geometry on each claimed conflicting hit from this ZIP**. Do not describe these statuses as independent re-certification of geometric contradictions.

**F-04 — Limited no-request/output identity and power evidence (not a defect in the narrow experiment).** The runner asserts no `finiteHitDiagnostic` key in raw or accepted observations for every OFF execution, checks scene serialization before/after, and requires ON's `nativeMeasurementQualificationUnchanged:true`; the recorded OFF advisory is `null` and ON failed runs are suppressed. The independently verified `nativeFingerprint` is a **projection of selected counters/outcomes and detector totals**, not a bit-for-bit comparison of full canonical no-request reports or a full per-ray power ledger. Normal-control detector signed-power summaries agree, but this matrix **cannot establish exact native-ray/power identity or observation neutrality for unobserved cases**. Existing production tests at PR #89 remain a separate evidence line.

**F-05 — Source interpretation and preservation limits.** All nine cases are **new explicitly synthetic authored scenes**, not nine independently interpreted textbook problems. The study tests neither held-out source interpretation, student usefulness, continuous illumination nor the original case's answer. The temporary research CI upload has **14-day artifact retention**: retain the verified original bytes and digests in a separately approved durable evidence location **before expiration** if this result will underpin later policy; do not silently treat an expiring Actions artifact as a permanent archive.
