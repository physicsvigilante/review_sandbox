## Design under review

Candidate **B1** is a **versioned two-independent-native-run finite-detector window measurement** with a **clearly ADVISORY post-native two-line geometric intersection** when well-conditioned. It does NOT claim an eye, pupil, retina, image sensor, physical apparent image, optical observer position, unique image, simultaneous two-ray observation, native backward-propagated ray, continuous irradiance or completed JEE textbook answer.

A proposed `opticsim-detector-selected-pair-request-v1` supplies a single **existing full v5 minimal authoring apparatus** with one point source (one explicitly authored direction), one native planar boundary, one finite detector, stable IDs and physical data; and **secondDirectionDeg** explicitly authored. Renderer/solver runs real PhyDemo for both directions after immutably cloning canonical apparatus and changing exactly that one direction field. There is no user-provided detector event and no new v20 scene. The proposal uses a **new standalone CLI**, not the historically byte-frozen `scripts/run-problem.mjs` or `scripts/author-scene.mjs`.
