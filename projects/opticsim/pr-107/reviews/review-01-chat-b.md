## Independent geometry findings

1. **Conservative separation is physically safe for strictly convex bodies (no confirmed bug).** The code validates each quadrilateral with the unchanged v9 convexity and 1 mm edge/altitude rules, then checks whether all vertices of one convex polygon lie at least 0.01 mm beyond an *outward supporting face* of the other (in either direction). Such a full separating-face certificate entails disjoint convex hulls and a projected separation of at least 0.01 mm. The polygon-face normal uses the sign of its winding, so reversing clockwise/counterclockwise order does not invert the physical outward test. The independent reference's 10,000 random rotated/reversed-winding rectangle pairs produced **0 unsafe admissions**, with 8,065 certified and 1,935 intersecting cases refused. This sample is not a proof for arbitrary floating-point pathological inputs; the convex-hull supporting-plane argument establishes the geometric implication.
2. **Deliberate false refusal, not overlap admission:** for axis-aligned 20 × 20 mm squares centred at `(0,0)` and `(20.008,20.008)`, Shapely's shortest distance is **0.011313708 mm** (more than 0.01 mm), but neither square provides a full supporting-face projection >= 0.01 mm, so the candidate rejects it. This is consistent with its explicitly conservative contract; do not secretly relax the clearance or call it a physics bug.
3. **Near-touch and invalid bodies:** 20 × 20 mm squares with a 0.005 mm face gap reject, 0.011 mm face gap admit, touching/intersecting and nested arrangements reject; a pair of separated rectangles at +37° admits in either winding. Source exactly on G2's left face `(200,0)` has zero finite-edge distance and is rejected by the reused v9 source check; forward-ray vertex targeting and detector-outside checks also remain in that validator. Source `(300,10)` inside G2 is not itself forbidden and is covered by source CI. The v14 source tests additionally refuse extra optics, mismatched exterior indices and old-schema leakage.
