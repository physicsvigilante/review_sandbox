## Physics and numeric audit — independently calculated

**Convention:** detector ordered finite endpoints p1→p2 give tangent `t=(dx,dy)/L`; positive left normal `n=(-t_y,t_x)` in the project's x-right/y-up frame. A native incoming unit direction with positive/negative dot product against `n` belongs to the corresponding *detector-relative* group. The code never associates a group with a particular source, or pairs crossings into a path. Statistics are *unweighted sample* properties, with `RMS = sqrt(sum((s_i-mean(s))^2)/N)` (population denominator N, centered residuals).

**Planar n=1.8 / mirror / horizontal detector:** from the actual fixture, the first ray crosses y=50 descending at 60° to the vertical, and the refracted ray has `theta=asin(sin(60°)/1.8)=28.75891800395°`. Independently:
- First hit: `x=-50 tan(60°)=-86.60254037844 mm`; incoming `u·n=-0.5`, so **negative normal**. Detector p1=(-225,50); distance from p1 = `138.39745962156 mm`.
- Reflected return hit: `x=50 tan(60°)+120 tan(theta)=152.46109637226 mm`; incoming `u·n=+0.5`, so **positive normal**. Distance = `377.46109637226 mm`.
- Archived native points `(-86.60254037844382,50)` and `(152.46109637225754,50)` agree to floating-point rounding. Both groups have N=1, mean equal to their own crossing, and sampled RMS=0; this does **not** imply a zero-width physical image. Output is correctly **qualified** by the explicit transmitted-only/Fresnel-off warning.

**Ideal lens/mirror / vertical detector:** the native artifact contains 11 raw events; finite detector p1=(-120,-110) to p2=(-120,110), `t=(0,+1)`, `n=(-1,0)`. Therefore **six incoming +x events are negative-normal** and **five incoming -x events are positive-normal**, not the reverse. Recomputing from the 11 raw `distanceFromP1Mm` records rather than from summary values gives:

**45° independent synthetic reference:** for center (15,-8), L=100 mm and tangent `(sqrt(1/2),sqrt(1/2))`, left normal is `(-sqrt(1/2),+sqrt(1/2))`. Distances 10/20/30 in the positive group give mean 20 mm, min/max 10/30, span 20 and population RMS `sqrt(200/3)=8.1649658093 mm); centroid **(-6.2132034356,-29.2132034356) mm**. Distances 50/70 in the negative group give mean 60, span 20, RMS 10 and centroid **(22.0710678119,-0.9289321881) mm**. For a vertical detector p1→p2 upward the left normal is -x; for a horizontal detector rightward it is +y.
