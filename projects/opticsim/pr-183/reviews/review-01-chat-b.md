## Focused physics and integration findings

### F1 — Versioned authoring and saved-schema isolation: PASS

[Composition profiles](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/6ea3c2edb4440e4e27b2f88f43660bd6ffed974f/src/composition-profiles.js) add five **separately keyed, exact-cardinality** compositions: v14 two disjoint convex finite Glass bodies, v15 1–2 finite biconvex SphericalLens bodies, v16 thick lens plus finite mirror, v17 two thick lenses plus mirror, and v18 those three plus a centred-gap finite Aperture. Their source modes are exact SingleRay; added objects are not silently admitted to v19–v24 Beam or v24 right-half Glass cases. V24's flat closing face, internal detector and absorber arrangement remains its own profile.

[Contract validation](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/6ea3c2edb4440e4e27b2f88f43660bd6ffed974f/src/contract.js) ties model, schemaVersion and adapterVersion together; the SphericalLens component type is admitted **only** in v15–v18. The v18 Aperture permission is version-limited; v17/v18 alone can use maxRayDepth 16, all other versions remain capped at 8. [Authoring](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/6ea3c2edb4440e4e27b2f88f43660bd6ffed974f/src/authoring.js) assigns deep policies only to exact versions and refuses a foreign explicit deep policy. The composition selection order chooses a candidate without repairing it by dropping disallowed objects or downgrading to a permissive profile. Actual mixed-family, version-forgery, preserve-v20-y, and v22/v23/v24 tests were inspected and passed in CI. No schema migration is inferred.

### F2 — Actual native material and face measurements: PASS within admitted physical scope

The v14 native finite Glass implementation and the v15–v18 spherical-lens geometry validators are **byte-identical** to their previously reviewed standalone modules, so those standalone physics reviews were not repeated. The *new integration paths* were checked: [SphericalLens compiler](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/6ea3c2edb4440e4e27b2f88f43660bd6ffed974f/src/spherical-lens.js) emits a genuine pinned-native SphericalLens with native y-down endpoints, front positive radius, back **negative** native radius, positive physical thickness, and inside/outside *relative* refractive index, not a thin-lens substitute. Admission validates edge thickness, clearances, medium indices, exact source positions and detector separation.

[Shared interface observer](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/6ea3c2edb4440e4e27b2f88f43660bd6ffed974f/src/planar-media.js) takes actual native hit coordinates and ray directions, constructs only **measurement** face IDs and local normals for front arc, back arc, top and bottom rims, and calls the original native incident delegate exactly once. Ambiguous/nonfinite/near-corner face evidence contributes a fatal observation error; this observer does not solve Snell/Fresnel optics or manufacture a ray. Native branches, misses, reflection and TIR are native outcomes. [Observable contracts](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/6ea3c2edb4440e4e27b2f88f43660bd6ffed974f/src/observables.js) expose only bounded sampled face/incidence/direction and detector evidence, with explicit Fresnel-off transmitted-only warnings; no reconstructed physical image or arbitrary path claim appears.

### F3 — Mandatory v18 post-refraction aperture-corner fail-closed gate: PASS (focused primary gate)

Inspected [actual immutable guard](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/6ea3c2edb4440e4e27b2f88f43660bd6ffed974f/src/native-v18-aperture-corner-guard.js), [v18 composition admission](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/6ea3c2edb4440e4e27b2f88f43660bd6ffed974f/src/thick-lens-aperture-composition.js), [adapter qualification](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/6ea3c2edb4440e4e27b2f88f43660bd6ffed974f/src/adapter.js), [native observer tests](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/6ea3c2edb4440e4e27b2f88f43660bd6ffed974f/test/native-v18-aperture-corner-guard.test.mjs) and [secondary-corner probe](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/6ea3c2edb4440e4e27b2f88f43660bd6ffed974f/test/native-v18-secondary-aperture-corner-probe.test.mjs).

- Guard attaches **only** for schema v18 with the exact v18 model, **after** ordinary native/source/interface/mirror observers, and wraps the actual instance-local SphericalLens/mirror onRayIncident callbacks. It invokes the pre-existing delegate **once, first**, returns that same result, and then *reads* mutated outgoing primary and actual newRays; no ray is changed or redirected. The test-only guard-bypass comparison checks native processed/emitted counts, lens face observations, mirror reflections, aperture absorption and detectors for equality, with and without the observer; this bypass is **not** part of production.
- The four physical aperture endpoints are converted from authored y-up to native y-down. For every outgoing ray state, the audit checks forward-only projection and perpendicular *line* distance to each endpoint. An observed distance **<= 0.01 mm**, uninspectable state, nonfinite/degenerate ray, invalid newRays collection, missing native incident handler or auditor capacity exhaustion fails **closed**. Conservative false-positive withholding is acceptable. No inferred aperture impact, absorption order or numerical exactness is asserted.
- Independent inspection of the archived native v17 reference at **x=50 mm** found the genuine right-going refracted detector sample **y=5.617799867730811 mm**, not the original source height. The v18 archived aperture gap-endpoint shifts have these results:

These are real pinned-native recorded trajectories, **not an analytically imposed route**. Raw native detector/absorption counts may still exist inside a diagnostic report for failed cases; that does **not** make them qualified observations.

- [App runtime](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/6ea3c2edb4440e4e27b2f88f43660bd6ffed974f/src/app.js) and [CLI checker](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/opticsim/blob/6ea3c2edb4440e4e27b2f88f43660bd6ffed974f/skills/sim-ro-image-to-scene/scripts/check-scene.mjs) both attach the guard and pass it to the same diagnostics. Guard errors enter diagnostics.errors and force classifyRun to **failed**, even if PhyDemo emits simulationComplete. This withholds detector-hit events and all external qualified observations. The near-corner native status is never represented as a certified arm/gap classification.

**Numerical limit:** A necessary forward ray-to-endpoint proximity test with a finite tolerance is a conservative safety **withholding** criterion, not a universal floating-point or complete ray-history proof. It is appropriately limited to the frozen v18 contract; changing the guard or admitting other geometries/depths would require new evidence and review.

Run classification fails on observer errors, stops on stop event, and marks truncation/undefined behavior incomplete. - **No universal numerical certification:** The post-refraction corner guard is deliberately conservative; an apparent native completion is not proof of a correct epsilon-scale aperture arm/gap classification in other unsampled geometries. Do not advertise those as solved or measured. A later change to numerical tolerance, native callback behavior, ray types, or v18 composition requires a separately pinned review.
- **No wider physical claim:** The v14–v18 additions remain constrained real-native optical arrangements, not a generic mixed optical graph, wave/diffraction engine, full Fresnel radiometry guarantee, native physical-image detector, or blind JEE answer solver. Fresnel partial reflection disabled is an explicitly documented approximation; TIR remains native.
- **Minor optional UI polish (not a blocker):** The common SingleRay guidance can still mention a cone-angle/opening field even when that field is inactive. The model-specific labels and physics are correct, and the Chrome suites pass; make the disabled-field explanatory sentence less prominent in later normal UI maintenance if desired.
- **Review evidence is specific:** Git-object and archive checks above are independently repeated. Native/browser physics outputs were inspected from genuine final CI, **not independently rerun locally**. Neither the builder's PASS nor this verdict implies independent calibration of every ray and every original source problem.
