## 3. Independent reflection-group and numerical verification

- Re-derived the four-reflection transformation rather than accepting the committed expected vector. For the canonical equilateral A=`(0,L/2)`, B=`(0,-L/2)`, C=`(sqrt(3)L/2,0)`, composition **chronologically** `AB → BC → AB → CA` has linear matrix **I** and translation `t=(sqrt(3)L/2, 3L/2)`. Its norm is `sqrt(3)L`, strictly nonzero for `L>0`. Consequently `W^k(p)=p+k t` consists of infinitely many distinct *formal* positions for **every** source point p, including the centroid and points on a median. This is a direct affine-reflection proof; no native path, finite segment, eye or intensity follows from it.
- For the explicitly synthetic 140-mm fixture the independently derived shift is approximately `(121.2435565298214, 210)` mm with norm `242.4871130596428` mm. An independent exact symbolic calculation confirmed the linear identity and shift; iteration six times from the centroid gave the expected distinct translates. Rigid rotation, translation or positive uniform scaling of the entire triangle conjugates/scales the witness and preserves its nonzero character.
- Independently reimplemented point reflection and breadth-first position deduplication and compared against the downloaded authored numeric evidence: at `maxReflectionOrder=5` the first-depth layers are **1, 3, 6, 9, 12, 15** (depth 0 includes the original object); the 45 non-original points, first-depth labels and recorded coordinates match within numerical tolerance. These are **finite-depth formal candidate points only**, never the full orbit, ray path lineage, physical image set or visible-image total.
- Source-only `equilateral-three-mirror-orbit-structure` needs only format/kind/model/topology/unlimited-reflection assertions and returns conditional unbounded formal cardinality, `physicalVisibleImageCount:null`, `numberOfVisibleImagesCertified:false`, `native.status:'not-run'`, and no invented metric source/vertices.
- Separately authored `equilateral-three-mirror-bounded-formal-points` requires three ordered vertices, explicit interior source and integer depth 0–8. Implementation checks the relative equilateral side tolerance `1e-9`, 10–2000 mm mean side, input coordinates within ±10000 mm, source distance strictly exceeding 0.01 mm from each supporting side, and bounded distinct-point deduplication at `1e-7` mm. It refuses unsupported fields, missing facts, wrong mode/policy, boundary/outside source, malformed numeric values or depths outside the admitted interval. Tests cover these negative paths; tolerance-based dedup should not be presented as exact mathematical equality near near-symmetric coincidences.
