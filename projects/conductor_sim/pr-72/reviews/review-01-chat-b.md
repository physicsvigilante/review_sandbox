## Findings

### F-01 — BLOCKER, evidence integrity: root timestamp may be relabeled without reproducing its physical state

**Source**: [`motion_events.py` lines 450–520](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/conductor_sim/blob/7c177e7da153992d963c966091c76ed12faab5a0/src/conductor_sim/motion_events.py#L450-L520), specifically lines 465–477 and 493–513; [`coupled_event_family.py` lines 43–53, 114–125, 151–175](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/conductor_sim/blob/7c177e7da153992d963c966091c76ed12faab5a0/src/conductor_sim/coupled_event_family.py#L43-L53).

`validate_event_run` verifies that a saved root has a bounded bracket, correct signed geometry, a low spatial residual, and internally consistent force/energy **at the root's stored position**. It regenerates `_sample(spec, row.time_s, position, velocity)`, but static fields and energy do not tie that position/velocity to the claimed timestamp or its integration trajectory. `EventRun` and `LocatedEvent` are immutable dataclasses for ordinary assignments, yet `dataclasses.replace` constructs exact built-in instances with changed fields. Consequently, shifting a genuine located event's `sample.time_s` and both bracket endpoints by **+0.030 s**, retaining its old position and velocity, appears to preserve the checked invariants while altering exported crossing time and subsequent parent→child shift. For the verified structural parent, independent integration shows its genuine crossing at `0.55293327517 s`; 30 ms later it is at x≈−0.008205566 m, **not** the declared x=−0.006 m detector.

**Evidence type:** source-derived predicted bypass plus independently evaluated dynamics; **NOT a successful native forged-object execution**. A runnable native test is provided in `review-01-chat-b-probes.py` as `FORGED-ROOT-TIME`. The frozen charter explicitly requires rejection of invented timestamp/root. **Smallest remedy:** bind every located event's time/state to independently checked native trajectory/step evidence (or a verified event ledger / rerun), rather than relying only on self-consistent position-plane and force fields. Add a regression that shifts both root time and bracket while retaining the native state, at constructor and before export. Do not call a local root bracket a bound on exact ODE error.

### F-02 — BLOCKER, evidence integrity: an authentic observed structural parent can be reclassified as an unobserved refusal

**Source:** [`coupled_event_family.py` lines 132–154](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/conductor_sim/blob/7c177e7da153992d963c966091c76ed12faab5a0/src/conductor_sim/coupled_event_family.py#L132-L154), [lines 203–209](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/conductor_sim/blob/7c177e7da153992d963c966091c76ed12faab5a0/src/conductor_sim/coupled_event_family.py#L203-L209).

For an admitted structural parent with ordinary trajectory, the constructor accepts `p.event_run is None` whenever `p.status` is one of `rejected-event-study` or `integration-failed`. It does **not** verify that the given `EventStudy` actually refused or failed. A `dataclasses.replace` of an authentic successful `CoupledParentEvent` into `event_run=None, status='rejected-event-study'` appears acceptable, and export then replaces a genuine eligible parent→child root shift with `unpaired`. A free-form `reason` cannot authenticate a genuine M24a failure. A similar substitution may be possible on admitted children.

**Evidence type:** predicted exact-built-in-result constructor bypass on source inspection; **not independently executed**. The probe `FORGED-PARENT-REFUSAL` reproduces the intended challenge, if native runtime is available. **Smallest remedy:** preserve an authenticated native observation-result/refusal ledger within the trusted runner or make construction of accepted-result/refusal variants controlled. Check original M24a failure provenance at construction/export; reject an externally substituted refusal without genuine underlying native failure. Test parent and child, post-construction replacement where applicable.

### F-03 — MAJOR / release gate, physical vs event-study status: child event integration is attempted after the M31a ordinary physical integration failed

**Source:** [`coupled_event_family.py` lines 279–285](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/conductor_sim/blob/7c177e7da153992d963c966091c76ed12faab5a0/src/conductor_sim/coupled_event_family.py#L279-L285); [`coupled_physical_family.py` lines 344–363](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/conductor_sim/blob/7c177e7da153992d963c966091c76ed12faab5a0/src/conductor_sim/coupled_physical_family.py#L344-L363).

M31a can produce an entry with non-null `entry.scenario`, **null** `entry.trajectory` and `entry.status='integration-failed'`. M31b branches only on `entry.scenario is None`, so **it calls `_observe(entry.scenario,...)` even when the physical world was not numerically solved by M31a**. Different event-specific time policies could produce a successful event run despite the plain-run failure, leaving a contradictory `physical_status='integration-failed'` / `event_status='complete'` pair. The frozen charter says a child that M31a did not admit/**solve** should not receive fabricated event evidence. The constructor likewise does not enforce null event observation when `physical.trajectory is None`.

**Evidence type:** observed source-code control flow and predicted conflicting output; an input that triggers both sides has **not** been executed. **Smallest remedy:** explicitly distinguish `scenario is not None and trajectory is None` from ordinary physical success. Preserve the physical integration-failed classification and either suppress M24a event study or formally amend the contract to describe an independent numerical rescue with clear separate qualification and rigorous tests. Gate the typed constructor accordingly. Add one deterministic physical-failure case before release.
