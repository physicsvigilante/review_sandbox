## Existing engine reused and new missing layer

M22a already provided atomic `NamedSource`, `SourceReplacement`, `reassemble_scenario`, and `ScenarioReassembly`, with **retained identity ancestry and entire-model physics qualification** after all edits. M28a compared fully qualified **parameter-only** revisions; M28b compared typed physical detector events across parameter revisions. M29a adds no new electric or gravitational field law, no induced-charge conductor solver, no RK, event locator, numerical guard, 3D free-motion qualification or browser-side physics. It fills the gap of comparing **genuinely different source inventories** after atomic structural reassembly.

New files `src/conductor_sim/structural_family.py` and `structural_family_viewer.py`, public exports, `examples/m29a_structural_family.py`, `tests/test_m29a_structural_family.py`, `tools/check_m29a_browser.py`, charters/docs and a new real Chromium acceptance step.

- `StructuralCase` is one explicit bounded structural operation (1..12 requested add/remove/replace/gravity membership operations), with a stable `case_id`, teacher note, tuples of existing qualified `NamedSource`/`SourceReplacement`, or explicit gravity_action `keep/add/remove`. No same-ID physical property edit is allowed (use M20b/M28a for that), no arbitrary source classes or callbacks, no changed source guard/duration/time grid/plane during a structural operation.
- `StructuralFamilyPlan` has **one unchanged PrescribedScenario parent** and 1..5 distinct stable case IDs. Each child is **independently** assembled from the same original parent (never from sibling), via unchanged M22a `reassemble_scenario`, then **freshly integrated** through unchanged `integrate_test_charge`. The resulting `ScenarioReassembly` record is retained, not replaced with a misleading parameter-revision ledger. Source replacement **retires old ID**, with a new binding at its slot and a before/after ledger. Actual source labels are author assertions, not PDF verification.
- `rejected-structural-model`, `integration-failed`, `censored` and `complete` are distinct visible states. If structural model is physically inadmissible (e.g. an unpaired off-plane point), no fake trajectory is reported; unrelated qualified siblings still integrate. A runtime source guard retains real last time and no postguard extrapolation.
- The `conductor_sim.structural_motion_family.v1` detached JSON holds baseline source IDs/revision and original native history, every true structural parent/child lineage/revision hash and membership change, each qualified child history or explicit refusal, and component/Euclidean **3D Δr and Δv only at literally identical saved physical timestamps**. A terminal guard may contribute a non-grid final sample with no aligned baseline time. Source inventories and potential references differ: *do not infer cross-inventory electrostatic work from ΔU*. Model SHA is record identity, not source PDF authentication or solver proof.
- Offline `render_structural_family` projects genuine particle paths into the **unchanged certified physical motion plane** and runs a shared **discrete union of actual saved timestamps** slider. The per-row table shows *actual* source inventory, source type/name, gravity membership, structural edits and typed status. The chart is explicit **trajectory space only** — NOT source-rim/point/rod apparatus geometry, a moving source, conductor backreaction, collision or browser ODE. Actual apparatus views remain in existing M22a/M27b viewers. Author-provided model origins/labels are safely escaped and not a made-up universal workbook attribution.
