- A cavity of radius a, centre c, fixed source charges q_j at x_j with d_j=|x_j-c|<a has metal-side inner surface density at outward-from-cavity-centre *unit normal* n:

  **sigma_inner(n) = -SUM_j [ q_j (a²-d_j²) / (4π a |a n-(x_j-c)|³) ]**, units **C/m²**.
