## Decision and blocking findings

**R2-01 — finite, nonzero source field exported as missing/zero direction (BLOCKER).** The public strict scene authoring parser accepts gravity uniform field [1e200, 0, 0] m/s² (also [1e-200, 0, 0] m/s²). The true sampled field values are finite and nonzero in every probe. However, src/emfeeld/visualization.py uses np.linalg.norm(field_vectors, axis=1) to decide validity and normalize the vectors. At 1e200 the square-sum overflows, and the portable field.schematic_arrows.unit_directions is [0,0,0] for 343 samples; at 1e-200 it underflows and all 343 nonzero directions are omitted. This breaks the new FieldSamples promise of unit directions at each valid nonzero sample, even though the physical field and potential remain preserved. The same arithmetic limitation existed in the legacy viewer; the issue here is the newly committed renderer-neutral contract emitting semantically false data for an admitted input. An overflow warning is also produced, incompatible with warnings-as-errors qualification for that case. Reproducer: reviewer_pr52_blockers.py, results52/blockers.json. Correct only the presentation normalization/validity algorithm (e.g. scaled robust vector norm/direction), or explicitly reject unsupported numeric presentation ranges without misrepresenting successful directions. Add tests for both magnitudes and a mixed-sign nonaxial vector; do not change the field law.

**R2-02 — standalone recorded snapshot accepts inconsistent sample count (BLOCKER).** Calling build_visualization(scene, recorded=...) with a declared 5-sample scene and a supplied two-position path succeeds, exports animation.samples=5 and a path of length two, then data.frame(4) raises IndexError. This contradicts the documented portable saved-frame identity and can reach a second renderer that directly calls the new builder. The higher-level make_plotter performs validate_recorded, but the new standalone builder does not. Reproducer: reviewer_pr52_blockers.py, results52/blockers.json. Validate at least each recorded path's shape, finiteness, and length against scene.samples at the builder boundary (or enforce an equivalent validation precondition there) and give a typed early refusal. Keep valid recorded playback unchanged; no solver change required.

These are bounded presentation contracts, not a demand to reopen engine architecture, authoring grammar or the specialized Gauss/cavity/dipole viewers.
