## F2: Axis-only probe admission tightened

The old `64*eps*(norm(point)+norm(centre)+norm(displacement))` radial allowance was removed. For an axis-aligned normal, all transverse coordinates must match the centre **exactly**, regardless of whether the displaced coordinate is 1 or 7 representable ULPs away. In particular the exact reviewer example `centre=(100,0,0)`, `normal=(0,0,1)`, `point=(100+1e-13,0,0)` now raises `ValueError("off-axis ... not implemented")` for positive, negative and zero currents.

For a rotated normal, the implementation tries to reconstruct the input from an axial z and checks coordinate-by-coordinate against **at most two float64 ULPs** at the maximum absolute original/probe/reconstructed component. Exactly zero normal components still require exact coordinate identity. This documented allowance is only for binary64 construction/rotation rounding, not an off-axis physical model or a general Biot–Savart solver. Independent displaced rotated probes at 1e-8 m are refused; 500 deterministic random rotated/translated near-axis probes still compute correct axial SI values.
